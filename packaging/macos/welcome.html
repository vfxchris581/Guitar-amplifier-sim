Guitar Amplifier Sim

This installs the VST3 plug-in into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 for all users. Open your DAW afterwards and rescan plug-ins if it doesn't appear.

Your models, captures, impulse responses and presets are kept in Documents/Guitar Amplifier Sim; installing or updating never touches them.

Includes open-source software (Neural Amp Modeler core, Eigen, JUCE and others); see "Third-Party Notices" on the disk image.
